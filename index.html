High-Tech Printing

The DTF Advantage

Direct to Film technology is transforming custom apparel. Here is why it beats screen printing and standard HTV vinyl.

Unlimited Colors

Prints crisp, photorealistic, full-color designs including fine gradients and shadows. No extra charge per color!

High Stretch & Durability

Our DTF inks bond deep into fabric fibers. The print stretches with the garment and won't crack or peel under pressure.

Zero Minimum Order

Need just one custom shirt? No problem! Screen printing requires expensive screen setup, but DTF requires zero setup cost.

Prints on All Fabrics

Works flawlessly on 100% Cotton, Polyester, Blends, Canvas, Denim, and Nylon - regardless of shirt color!
